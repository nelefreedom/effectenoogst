Effectenoogst - marketing products experiments

These are concepts that I've created with Claude Fable 5.2 on November 12th 2026. I've given the machine my context to work with: all of my outcome harvesting materials, from theory to reports I've deveoped in the past. Also, I've given it my most recent proposal for the Initiatieven Collectief, a network of initiatives that support youth who sit at home, for an assigment that has just started for Movisie. The markdown files at the bottom contain the latest formulations. I tweaked the texts only once. The dashboard uses fictitious examples. Voices and images in the demo are produced with Elevenlabs, another AI tool for voice and video.
